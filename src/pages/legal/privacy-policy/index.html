Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Tacliimi Learn may collect, use, and protect information when you use the platform, including course learning, mentorship, account, communication, and payment-related features.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide information such as your name, email address, profile information, account credentials, course activity, mentorship requests, session information, messages or support requests, and information needed to complete a transaction.

3. Information About Platform Use

Tacliimi may process technical and usage information needed to operate and secure the service, such as browser or device information, page activity, authentication events, and error or security information. The exact information available may depend on the feature and technical service being used.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide courses and mentorship, maintain learning and session records, process or verify transactions, communicate with users, provide support, prevent abuse and fraud, improve platform functionality, and meet legal or security obligations.

5. Payments and Third-Party Services

When you make a payment or use another integrated service, relevant information may be processed by the third-party provider required for that service. Tacliimi does not need to store your payment credentials when those credentials are entered directly into a third-party payment service. The provider's own privacy and service terms may also apply.

6. Sharing of Information

Tacliimi may share information with service providers that help operate the platform, such as hosting, database, authentication, communications, analytics, or payment services, where necessary for the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform, or others.

7. Student and Mentor Information

Some profile and session information may be visible to other users when needed for a course, mentorship, communication, or booking feature. Users should avoid publishing sensitive personal information in public profile fields or messages unless it is necessary for the service.

8. Security

Tacliimi uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their passwords and account access.

9. Data Choices and Requests

If you have a question about your personal information, want to request correction, or need help with an account-related privacy matter, contact Tacliimi at tacliimiLearn@gmail.com. We may need to verify ownership of the relevant account before acting on a request.

10. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Tacliimi's features, services, or legal requirements change. The current version and its last-updated date will be published on this page.

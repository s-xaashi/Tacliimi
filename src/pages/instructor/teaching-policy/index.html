Teaching Policy

These guidelines help keep courses on Tacliimi useful, respectful, and safe for learners.

1. Educational quality

Course descriptions, learning objectives, and lesson content should accurately represent what students will learn. Organize material in a clear sequence and make reasonable efforts to keep it current and understandable.

2. Originality and permissions

Only upload or share videos, documents, images, quizzes, and other materials that you created or have permission to use. Do not infringe copyrights, trademarks, privacy, or other people's rights.

3. Respectful learning environment

Communicate professionally with students and other instructors. Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, misleading claims, and abusive conduct are not acceptable.

4. Honest course information

Provide accurate details about course level, requirements, included resources, and pricing. Do not make false promises about qualifications, outcomes, certificates, or employment opportunities.

5. Review and compliance

Courses may be reviewed before publication. Instructors should respond to review feedback and correct issues before resubmitting. Tacliimi may restrict or remove content that violates platform rules or applicable law.

Questions about this policy

Contact Tacliimi Support or email tacliimiLearn@gmail.com if you need clarification.

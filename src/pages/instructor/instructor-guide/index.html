Instructor Guide

Use this guide to prepare courses, manage your teaching content, and support your students on Tacliimi.

1. Create a course

Open My Courses and select the option to create a course. Add a clear title, category, description, learning objectives, and a suitable thumbnail so students understand what they will learn.

2. Organize your curriculum

Divide your course into logical sections and lessons. Give each lesson a descriptive title, check that videos and resources belong to the intended lesson, and preview the curriculum before moving to the next step.

3. Review pricing and publishing

Check your selected pricing, course details, and content before submitting for review. A submission may need administrator approval before it becomes available to students. Review any feedback and make the requested corrections.

4. Keep course content accurate

When updating a published course, review your changes carefully and use Save to Draft when you want to keep work without submitting it. Make sure lesson order, links, quizzes, and downloadable resources are correct.

Need help?

If you have trouble creating a course, submitting for review, or managing your account, visit the Support Center or email tacliimiLearn@gmail.com.
